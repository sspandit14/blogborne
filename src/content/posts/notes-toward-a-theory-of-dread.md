Dread is not fear. Fear has an object; it knows what is coming up the stairs. Dread has only the stairs, and the sound, and the long wait to find out whether the sound was anything at all.

We seek it out anyway, in novels and films and games built for nothing else. Perhaps because dread chosen is dread contained, and there is relief in rehearsing a feeling we cannot otherwise control.
