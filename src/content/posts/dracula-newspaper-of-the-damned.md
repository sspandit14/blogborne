Stoker's novel is a dossier. Nobody narrates it; it is collected, typed up, cross-referenced and filed, and the horror arrives through the gaps between documents.

Read that way, it is less a gothic romance than an early piece of investigative journalism, with a vampire as the story nobody will print.
