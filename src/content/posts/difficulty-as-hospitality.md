A game that will not tell you what to do is making an assumption about you: that you are capable of finding out. It is a strange kind of compliment, and like most compliments it is easy to mistake for an insult.

The hand-holding tutorial is polite. The silent, punishing opening is hospitable in an older sense: it lets you into the house and trusts you not to need the rules explained.

There is a cost, of course. Not every guest wants to be trusted. Some want the light switched on and the bathroom pointed out, and there is nothing wrong with that. But the game that withholds is offering something the polite game cannot: the pleasure of having worked it out yourself.
