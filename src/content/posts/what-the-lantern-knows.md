Every hostile world needs one place that is not hostile. In games it is usually a fire, a lamp, a bench, a typewriter: somewhere the music changes and the enemies, for a moment, stop mattering.

What is interesting is how little these places offer. They heal you, they save your progress, and they send everything you killed back to life. Rest, in other words, is not free. It resets the world as well as you.

That trade is the whole design. The lantern does not make the world safer. It only makes it bearable to go back out.
