## What this is

Blogborne is a personal blog of long-form essays. It is not a newsletter, it has no comments section, and it does not track who reads it. Essays are published when they are finished, which is to say irregularly.

The name and the look borrow from two places: the gothic, lamplit mood of the things written about here, and the old broadsheet newspaper, with its mastheads, rules and small-caps labels.

## What gets written here

Mostly essays on **books**, **films**, **games** and **philosophy**, and the places where they overlap. Some essays are reviews of a single work; those carry an *Under Review* box naming it. Others wander.

Expect length. A typical essay is somewhere between two and five thousand words, written to be read in one sitting with a cup of something.

## Following along

The easiest way to follow new essays is the [RSS feed](../rss.xml), which works with any feed reader. Everything ever published is listed in the [Archives](../archives/).
