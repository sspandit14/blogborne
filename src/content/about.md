## What this is

A personal blog consisting of long-form essays, thoughts, reflections, and the like (usually to do with or inspired by a video game, movie, or book).

## Why "Blogborne"?

I just really like Bloodborne and its Victorian/Gothic aesthetic, and the title of Blogborne is oddly fitting... I also do just have a fondness for physical/tangible media, and so it was an excuse to very loosely (and kind of barely) mix the design of an old newspaper with modern news outlet sites.
